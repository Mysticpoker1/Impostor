Party games · 2 to 7+ players

Game Night

Two games, one room. The host picks which one to play.

  • Who's the Impostor? Everyone gets the same word except one of you. Hint, bluff, vote.
  • Who Am I? Everyone sees your card but you. Ask yes/no questions on voice chat until you get it.

Share the invite link (or the code). The first person in is the host, and the room closes if the host leaves.
