Party game · 3+ players

Who's the Impostor?

Everyone gets the same Egyptian word. Except one of you.

  • Take turns saying one word that hints at your secret word.
  • The impostor has no word and has to bluff.
  • Keep going round until everyone is ready, then vote.

Share the invite link (or the code). The first person in hosts, and the room closes if the host leaves.
